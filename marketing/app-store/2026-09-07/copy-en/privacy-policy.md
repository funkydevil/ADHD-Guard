## How the app works with your information

ADHD Guard evaluates your active app and, with your permission, the active tab URL in a supported browser. It uses this information on your Mac to apply your focus rules, show reminders and help you return to a previously focused app or website.

The app stores your focus-app list, distracting-site list, reminder settings, custom reminder phrases, reminder positions and a short recent activity list in its local settings. Recent activity can include app names, app identifiers, website domains and full page URLs, including their paths or query parameters. These values are not uploaded by ADHD Guard.

When you ask to return to a saved website, the app may inspect open tab URLs to find a matching tab. If no matching tab is found, it may ask your browser to reopen the saved URL. Visiting that page is then handled by your browser and the website.

## Permissions

macOS Automation permission lets ADHD Guard communicate with supported browsers for URL checks and focus return. You can manage this permission in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Automation.

When you choose an app for your focus list, ADHD Guard reads the selected application bundle's name and identifier.

For optional idle reminders, the app checks the time elapsed since keyboard and mouse events. It does not capture typed text. The app does not request microphone, screen recording, Accessibility or full-disk access.

## Transmission and third-party services

ADHD Guard does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not transmit your app or browsing activity to the developer. No account is required.

Apple's App Store, macOS services, your browser and the websites you visit operate under their own privacy practices. Backups and device-management tools you use may include local app data independently of ADHD Guard.

## Your controls and local storage

You can edit or remove individual focus rules and change your reminder settings in the app. Turning Focus mode off stops reminders, but recent activity continues to update locally while the app is running. Quit ADHD Guard to stop the app entirely.

Settings and recent activity persist across app restarts. The recent activity lists are limited in size, so newer entries replace older entries. Resetting settings is not a complete deletion of recent activity or all stored app data. To request instructions for removing the app's local stored data, contact [kp.gentle.support@gmail.com](mailto:kp.gentle.support@gmail.com). The developer cannot remotely access or delete data stored only on your Mac.
