# ADHD Guard Support

ADHD Guard helps you return to your task with focus reminders on your Mac.

Need help? Email [kp.gentle.support@gmail.com](mailto:kp.gentle.support@gmail.com). You can also open an issue on [ADHD Guard support on GitHub Issues](https://github.com/funkydevil/funkydevil.github.io/issues) and include ADHD Guard in the title.

Please include your macOS version, ADHD Guard version, browser name if relevant, and the steps that led to the issue. Share screenshots only if you are comfortable with their contents.

## Get started

1. Open ADHD Guard and go to Lists.
2. Under Focus apps, add the apps you want to work in.
3. Under Distracting sites, add websites that tend to pull you away.
4. In Settings, choose your Focus buffer and reminder preferences.
5. Turn on Focus mode and return to your work.

## Why does a reminder appear?

An app outside your focus list starts the reminder timer. A listed distracting website also starts it, even when the browser is in your focus list. The reminder appears once your Focus buffer runs out. Switching between distracting apps and sites does not restart the timer.

Return to a focus app or a non-distracting page in a focus browser to clear the distraction reminder. You can also turn Focus mode off.

## Why is a website reminder not appearing?

Use Safari or Google Chrome, turn Focus mode on, and make sure the site's domain is in Distracting sites. Keep the tab frontmost for longer than your Focus buffer.

Allow ADHD Guard to access the browser when macOS asks. If you previously denied access, open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Automation and enable the relevant browser under ADHD Guard. Settings also provides an Open Automation Settings button when browser access needs attention.

The browser must have an accessible active tab. Website checks need browser permission; app-based reminders can work without it.

## How do I change sound or speech?

Open Settings > Reminders. Enable or disable Chime and Spoken reminder independently. When speech is enabled, you can edit the Focus phrase and Idle phrase. Use Preview reminder to hear your choices.

## What does Return to focus do?

It returns to the last focused app or website observed by ADHD Guard. For a saved website, the app looks for a matching open tab and selects it. If that tab is no longer available, the browser may reopen the saved URL. First use a focus app or an allowed browser page so there is a destination to return to.

## What is the idle reminder?

It is an optional reminder after a configured period without keyboard or mouse activity while Focus mode is on and the current context is focused. It uses elapsed time since input events, without recording the text you type.

## Does turning Focus mode off stop recent activity?

Turning Focus mode off stops reminders. The short local recent activity list continues to update while the app is running. Choose Quit ADHD Guard from its menu bar menu to stop the app entirely.

## Where is my activity stored?

The app stores rules, settings, recent app names and identifiers, recent websites and associated URLs on your Mac. It does not upload app or browsing activity. See [the privacy policy](https://funkydevil.github.io/adhd-guard/privacy.html) for details.

## Can I start ADHD Guard automatically?

Enable Launch at login in Settings or the menu bar menu. If macOS requests approval, follow the app's Login Items prompt.
