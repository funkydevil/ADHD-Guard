Get back to what matters.

You open a tab for a quick break. You switch apps to check one thing. ADHD Guard helps you notice when that detour takes you away from your task.

Choose your focus apps and distracting websites, then turn on Focus mode. If you stay outside your focus apps or on a distracting site beyond your chosen buffer, a visible reminder prompts you to return. Optional sound and spoken reminders keep nudging you until you get back to focus.

CHOOSE YOUR FOCUS APPS
Build a list of the apps you want to work in. Add apps from recent activity or choose them from your Applications folder.

CATCH YOUR DISTRACTING SITES
Add the websites that tend to pull you away. Listed sites start the reminder timer even when your browser is a focus app. Matching subdomains are included.

GIVE YOURSELF A MOMENT
Set how long a detour can last before reminders begin. Switching between distracting apps and sites keeps the same timer running.

RETURN TO YOUR TASK
Use Return to focus to bring your last focused app or browser page back into view. Move the reminder banner to a spot that suits your workspace.

MAKE REMINDERS YOUR OWN
Choose a repeating chime, spoken reminders, or a visual reminder on its own. Write your own reminder phrases and preview them in Settings. Enable an optional idle reminder for periods without keyboard or mouse activity.

KEEP IT CLOSE
Turn Focus mode on or off from the menu bar, check recent activity, and choose to launch ADHD Guard when you log in.

YOUR ACTIVITY STAYS LOCAL
Focus rules, reminder settings, and a short recent activity list are stored on your Mac. The app does not upload your app or browsing activity. No account is required.

Website reminders work with Safari and Google Chrome and require macOS Automation permission.

For work, study, and the moments when you could use a reminder to come back.
