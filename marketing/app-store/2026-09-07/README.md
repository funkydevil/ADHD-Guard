# ADHD Guard — Mac App Store artwork

Four English marketing cards, in display order:

1. **Get back to what matters.** — reminder banner with Status as context.
2. **Choose your focus apps.** — focus application list.
3. **Catch your distractions.** — website entry and distracting website list.
4. **Reminders at your pace.** — timing, chime, and spoken reminder settings.

Design: warm ivory background, forest typography, teal emphasis, large readable headlines and enlarged app UI. Menu bar omitted because the source includes unrelated app icons and is low resolution. The website card omits recent activity containing technical URLs. The reminder leads the series because it best explains the product's purpose.

`en-US/` contains four opaque RGB PNG exports at 2560 × 1600. `originals/` preserves the unscaled imagegen output. Exports were resized from generated artwork; 2560 × 1600 is not native source detail. These are AI-composited marketing images based on the supplied screenshots, not pixel-identical screen captures: small typography, spacing and icon details have been redrawn. All four exports were uploaded to App Store Connect and verified as COMPLETE; see app-store-connect-receipt.json.

Mac screenshot dimensions were checked against [Apple's screenshot specifications](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications) on 2026-09-07. The 2560 × 1600 dimensions are accepted; this does not establish App Review acceptance.

The fourth card provides useful customization detail; the first three carry the core story. If reducing to three, update the numbering printed in the artwork.
